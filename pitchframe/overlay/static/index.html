Every match, on one sphere

Each card is one named match — generated from a seed, or recorded from StatsBomb's open data — with what the match contains written on it: the scoreline, how many goals, how many came from moves of five touches or more, and where in the ninety minutes those goals fell. Pick one and the feed opens on it — every line of that replay still carries its own measurement, because nothing here replaces the verification, it only chooses what to verify.

Every card names a whole match — a seed for a generated one, a feed id for a recorded one. The sphere chooses between replays that already exist rather than building anything on the way to one. The catalogue is served from the project's own registry, so a card cannot offer a match the feed has not measured.
