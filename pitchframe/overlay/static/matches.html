What is on,
and what happened

Two kinds of match, and the page is careful about the difference. A live match is a goal feed: the score, the clock and every goal with its scorer, refreshed while the match is in play. A recorded match is a full event stream from StatsBomb's open data, and opening one runs the verified replay this project is built around — every published line checked against the events.
